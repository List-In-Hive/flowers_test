# Assets

All seven floral images are AI-generated demonstration assets, created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-25. They are not photographs of real stock, a real florist's work or customer orders. No external photo hotlinks, third-party logos or purchased stock were used.

- `public/images/hero.webp`: generated 1536×1024 editorial image, converted to WebP.
- `public/images/arrangement-1.webp` through `arrangement-6.webp`: six 512×512 crops from one generated 3×2 catalogue sheet, converted to WebP.
- `app/icon.svg`: simple text favicon created in this project.

## Generation prompts

Hero: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: florist website editorial hero photograph. Landscape 3:2 composition. Abundant blush roses, ivory ranunculus, burgundy dahlias and eucalyptus arranged in a ceramic vase on a cream worktable, botanical green background. Soft natural window light, tactile petals, understated editorial photography, natural beauty. Show the whole arrangement attractively with breathing room. No text, logos or watermarks.

Catalogue: Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: florist website catalog contact sheet that will be cropped into six individual catalog images. Landscape 3:2 image, perfectly aligned three-column by two-row grid of exactly six distinct arrangements, each cell square and equally sized. Every whole arrangement centered inside its cell with generous margins on seamless ivory background. No gutters, no dividing lines, no text. Top row left to right: blush rose handtied bouquet; ivory white flowers in a vase; deep burgundy dahlia bouquet. Bottom row left to right: warm peach rose bouquet; lavender lilac bouquet; sunny yellow mixed bouquet. Photorealistic floral catalog photography, soft natural light, consistent scale and camera angle in all six cells, refined botanical foliage. Clear straight equal grid cells. No text, logos, watermarks, props, or flowers crossing cell boundaries.
