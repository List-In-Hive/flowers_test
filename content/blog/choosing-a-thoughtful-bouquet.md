The best place to start is the person, not the occasion. What colours do they wear? Is their home calm and minimal or full of pattern?

## Think about a feeling

Soft blush tones can feel gentle and understated. Deep burgundy brings drama. Sunny yellow feels bright and lively. These are design starting points, not rules about what flowers must mean.

## Consider where the flowers will live

A vase arrangement can be a thoughtful choice when the recipient may not have a suitable vase. A hand-tied bouquet leaves them free to arrange the stems their own way.

## Add something personal

Mention a favourite flower, a meaningful colour, or a memory you want the bouquet to evoke. Even a short note can help guide the design.
