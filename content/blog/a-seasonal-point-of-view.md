Part of the pleasure of flowers is that they change. Different shapes, colours and textures invite a fresh approach to an arrangement.

## Choose a palette before a recipe

A mood such as soft and airy or rich and expressive leaves room for the florist to work with available flowers. An exact stem list can be less flexible.

## Make room for texture

A beautiful composition is more than its largest blooms. Small accents, foliage and branching stems can add movement and give an arrangement its character.

## Ask what is available

Availability varies by location, supplier and time of year. Talk to your florist about what can be sourced for your preferred date, especially for a wedding or larger gathering.

## Agree the details together

If a particular flower matters to you, say so early. Discuss any substitutions before confirming your order.
