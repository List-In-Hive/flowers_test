A bouquet changes a room. A few thoughtful habits can help you enjoy it for longer.

## Start with a clean vase

Wash the vase before adding your flowers. Remove leaves that would sit below the waterline, and use clean scissors or flower snips to recut the stems.

## Give them a comfortable spot

Choose somewhere away from direct heat and strong sunlight. A bright windowsill might look lovely, but a cooler table is often a kinder home for cut flowers.

## Keep an eye on the water

Check the water regularly and refresh it when needed. Follow the instructions on any flower food supplied with your bouquet rather than guessing the amount.

## Enjoy the changes

Some flowers open slowly; others make their statement straight away. Remove spent stems as the arrangement changes and try a smaller vase for the blooms that remain.

Different stems have different needs. When you [enquire about an arrangement](/contact), ask for care notes for the flowers you choose.
